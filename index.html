HOW TO PLAY

WELCOME TO THE PIXEL WORLD!

You are trapped inside the Pixel World. To escape and return to the real world, you must collect enough COINS and complete three major areas:

> THE TRAP
> THE LINK
> THE KEY

But be careful. NULL is trying to steal your coins using deceptive digital situations. Some situations are REAL. Some situations are FAKE. You must investigate before making a decision.

HOW THE GAME WORKS

01
EXPLORE — Explore the Pixel World and interact with digital interfaces.
02
CHECK — Read messages, inspect senders, check links, websites and other details.
03
DECIDE — Choose what action to take based on the information you discovered.
04
PROTECT — Make safe decisions to protect your SAFETY SCORE and COINS.
05
ESCAPE — Collect enough COINS to unlock the EXIT GATE and escape the Pixel World.

PLAYER STATUS
